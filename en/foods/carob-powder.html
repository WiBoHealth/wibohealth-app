How many calories are in carob?

People in the Levant know carob from carob juice in Ramadan, or from carob molasses. Carob powder is roasted, ground carob pods, and it is brown like cocoa. There are 222 calories per 100 g, and a tablespoon (10 g) has about 22 calories and 4 g of fibre.

Does carob raise blood sugar?

Carob is naturally sweet because it contains natural sugars, but it also has a lot of fibre (40 g per 100 g!), and fibre slows absorption. But there is no approved published measurement of its glycemic index, so we will not give you a number of our own.

Carob or cocoa?

  • Carob is naturally sweet, so you need less sugar in the recipe. Cocoa is bitter.
  • Carob has no caffeine (while cocoa contains caffeine and theobromine), so it is fine in the evening and for children.
  • The taste is a little different: carob has a caramel and date flavour, not 100% chocolate.

How to use it

  • A hot drink: a spoon of carob in a cup of hot milk, without sugar or with just a little.
  • In oatmeal or yoghurt: it adds colour and a sweet taste.
  • In bread and cakes: you can replace half the cocoa with carob and cut the sugar.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of ready-made sweetened chocolate powder: carob with milk and no sugar.
  • Instead of cocoa at night: carob, because it has no caffeine.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in carob powder?

222 calories per 100 g, and a tablespoon (10 g) has about 22 calories.

Does carob raise blood sugar?

It contains natural sugars and a lot of fibre, but there is no approved published measurement of its index.

Does carob contain caffeine?

No, carob is caffeine-free, which is why many people use it instead of cocoa in the evening.

How do I use carob instead of cocoa?

Replace half or all of the cocoa with carob, and cut the sugar because carob is naturally sweet.
