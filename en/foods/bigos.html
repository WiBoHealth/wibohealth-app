Polish Bigos Hunter's Stew (Sauerkraut & Meat)

Soups & home-cooked dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Polish Bigos Hunter's Stew (Sauerkraut & Meat) (300 g) contains 495 kcal, with 9 g of net carbohydrates and 42 g of protein.

Serving advice

One plate (250-300 g) makes a complete winter lunch. You don't need bread or potatoes with it: the generous fibre and protein will keep you full for hours.

Best ways to prepare it

Choose lean or medium-fat cuts of meat and use less processed meat such as smoked sausage. Let the stew simmer gently until the sauerkraut fibres soften and become easier to digest.

Important nutrition note ⚠️

Cabbage fibre is an important part of a balanced diet. But watch the salt in ready-made sauerkraut, and rinse it briefly with water before cooking if you have fluid retention or high blood pressure.

Frequently asked questions

Is bigos fully suitable for keto and intermittent fasting?

It suits low-carb and keto diets: every 100 grams has only 3 grams of net carbs, with good protein and natural fats.

Does bigos affect my blood sugar reading after eating?

Its effect on blood sugar is very small, because the net carbs are low (9 g per portion), and the fibre (11.4 g per portion) and protein slow down absorption.

Is bigos good for the gut bacteria?

Yes, through its fibre: cabbage fibre ferments in the colon and produces short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs). Live bacteria, however, need fermented cabbage that hasn't been cooked, such as a spoonful of raw sauerkraut next to the dish.
