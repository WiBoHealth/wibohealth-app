Cooked Burdock Root (Gobo)

Vegetables

Quick answer

A medium portion of Cooked Burdock Root (Gobo) (100 g) contains 72 kcal, with 14 g of net carbohydrates and 1.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 30 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 4.2 (Low) for its actual effect.

Burdock root, known in Japan as gobo, has a special place in traditional Japanese cooking. These long, thin roots with brown skin and firm, fibrous flesh have a distinctive earthy, nutty flavour. They contain inulin fibre, chlorogenic acid and antioxidant polyphenols that feed beneficial colon bacteria and support regular bowel movements, with 3.3g of fibre and about 72 kcal per 100g, which makes burdock a good choice in blood-sugar-friendly diets.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A 100g portion makes an excellent warm side dish twice a week and suits insulin-resistance and type 2 diabetes diets, as well as anyone looking for gut-friendly fibre.

Best preparation methods

Scrape the skin with the back of a knife rather than peeling it all off (most antioxidants sit just underneath), cut into thin matchsticks and stir-fry with carrot, sesame oil and toasted sesame seeds (kinpira gobo).

Important Nutritional Note

Burdock has a mild diuretic effect and contains oxalates, so drink plenty of water and be careful if you have kidney stones. Buy it from a reliable source and do not forage it, because it can be confused with the roots of poisonous plants.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does burdock root help people with diabetes?

    Its glycemic index is low (30), the glycemic load of 100g is about 4.2, and its inulin fibre slows the absorption of sugars in the gut.

    Does burdock help clear skin and acne?

    Burdock is used in traditional medicine for skin problems, but there is not enough scientific evidence that eating it treats acne or eczema. Treat it as a nutritious vegetable, not a skin remedy.

    Is burdock good for weight loss?

    Yes. Its firm fibre needs longer chewing, and inulin adds to the feeling of fullness, all for moderate calories.
