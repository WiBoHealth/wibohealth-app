Italian Vanilla Panna Cotta with Berry Coulis

Panna cotta met vanille en rode bosvruchtensausSweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Italian Vanilla Panna Cotta with Berry Coulis (120 g) contains 266 kcal, with 21 g of net carbohydrates and 3.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 55 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 11.6 (Medium) for its actual effect.

The regal dessert from Piedmont whose name literally means “cooked cream”: fresh cream and milk are cooked with natural vanilla pods and then chilled until velvety and gently wobbly, and finished with a refreshing raspberry coulis with its natural tartness. The natural milk fat, together with the fibre and antioxidants of the berries, gives this dessert a low glycemic index (55), making it one of the gentlest desserts for your energy levels.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A great choice after fish or light meat dishes; its glycemic load (11.6) is among the best in the category of fine Western desserts.

Best preparation methods

Making the coulis by mashing fresh berries without cooking them with refined sugar keeps more vitamin C and noticeably lowers the carbohydrate in the sauce.

Important Nutritional Note

If you watch your total fat, replace half the cream with almond milk or skimmed milk and keep the gelatine, for the same lovely texture with less fat.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the gelatine in panna cotta add nutritional value?

    Gelatine is a protein derived from collagen, but a portion contains only a few grams, so it is not a meaningful nutrient source; its main role is to set the texture.

    Why is its glycemic index low?

    Because the added sugar is moderate and comes in a fatty, slowly absorbed base, together with berries rich in ellagic acid.

    Does it suit a low-carb diet?

    The traditional version contains about 21 g of available carbohydrate per glass, but made with a zero-calorie sweetener (such as erythritol) and fresh unsweetened berries it fits low-carb diets.
