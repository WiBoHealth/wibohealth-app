Kefir

Milk & dairy drinks

Quick answer

A medium portion of Kefir (250 g) contains 102 kcal, with 11.2 g of net carbohydrates and 8.2 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 36 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 4 (Low) for its actual effect.

A living source of probiotics: a fermented milk drink teeming with billions of beneficial bacteria that consume a large part of the lactose during fermentation, making it gentle on the gut, with a low glycemic index (36).

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One of the best things you can offer your digestive system every day; it supports a healthy gut microbiome, a healthy immune system and insulin sensitivity.

Best preparation methods

Drink a cold glass in the morning or between meals, and avoid commercial fruit-flavoured kefir, which is usually loaded with added sugar.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.
