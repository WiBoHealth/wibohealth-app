Yemeni Malawah Flaky Tandoor Flatbread with Ghee

Breads & pastries

Quick answer

A medium portion of Yemeni Malawah Flaky Tandoor Flatbread with Ghee (120 g) contains 372 kcal, with 54.6 g of net carbohydrates and 9 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 37.1 (High) for its actual effect.

The famous flaky Yemeni bread, baked in a tandoor and brushed with layers of country ghee and nigella seeds; crisp and layered like Egyptian feteer, with a high glycemic load (37.1) that calls for portion control.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One flatbread contains about 55 g of net carbohydrates, so sharing it and taking a quarter to half is the smartest choice for people with diabetes.

Best preparation methods

The nigella seeds on top add a distinctive flavour and a small amount of antioxidants.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.
