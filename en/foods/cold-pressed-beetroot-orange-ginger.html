Quick answer

A medium portion of Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice (200 g) contains 96 kcal, with 20.6 g of net carbohydrates and 2.2 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 54 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 11.1 (Medium) for its actual effect.

A crimson energiser for active people: a cold-pressed blend of beetroot, fresh orange rich in vitamin C and a warm kick of ginger, with a sweet, earthy, balanced taste.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

The nitrates in beetroot are converted into nitric oxide, which widens blood vessels, and studies suggest they may improve endurance performance.

Best preparation methods

Cold pressing limits heating and oxidation during preparation; drink it fresh, within a few hours.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice good for weight loss?

    Yes. Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice is very low in calories (48 kcal per 100 g), so you can eat a generous amount to add volume and fullness to a meal without many calories.

    How does Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 54 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 200 g portion has a glycemic load of 11.1 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice?

    A typical 200 g portion of Cold-Pressed Beetroot, Orange & Ginger Juice provides about 96 kcal, 2.2 g protein, 22.4 g carbohydrate, 0.4 g fat and 1.8 g fibre.

    When should you drink beetroot juice to improve sports performance?

    Drink it 90 to 120 minutes before training or running, when blood levels of nitrate and nitric oxide peak.
