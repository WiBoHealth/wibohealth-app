Bulgur

Grains & starches

Quick answer

A medium portion of Bulgur (150 g) contains 124 kcal, with 21.8 g of net carbohydrates and 4.7 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 47 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 10.2 (Medium) for its actual effect.

The hero of traditional Levantine cooking and one of the safest, most balanced grains, with a very low glycemic index (47) and an exceptional richness in fibre, iron and zinc that puts it ahead of most types of rice.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

The first choice and smartest alternative for people with diabetes in everyday dishes; it is absorbed very slowly and keeps you full for many hours.

Best preparation methods

Cook coarse bulgur with olive oil, onion and lentils (the classic mujaddara) or with tomatoes; combining olive oil and bulgur creates an ideal nutritional balance.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.
