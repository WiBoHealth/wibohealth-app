Pita Bread

PitabroodjeGrains & starches

Quick answer

A medium portion of Pita Bread (60 g) contains 165 kcal, with 31.7 g of net carbohydrates and 5.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 70 (High) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 22.2 (High) for its actual effect.

The king of the Middle Eastern table and of breakfast sandwiches, but because it is so thin and easy to eat, we often eat large amounts without noticing. Its high starch density gives it a high glycemic index (70), which calls for awareness of portion size and of how you fill it.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One loaf is roughly equal to two or three slices of toast bread; fill it 70% with vegetables and lean protein (such as eggs, fava beans or chicken) to reduce the share of bread.

Best preparation methods

Choose pita made from wholegrain or bran flour, and toast it in the oven with a little olive oil and za'atar instead of frying it.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pita Bread good for weight loss?

It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 60 g portion gives 165 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

How does Pita Bread affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 70 (High) according to Atkinson 2021. A typical 60 g portion has a glycemic load of 22.2 (High), so it is best to reduce or split the portion and combine it with protein and fibre.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Pita Bread?

A typical 60 g portion of Pita Bread provides about 165 kcal, 5.5 g protein, 33 g carbohydrate, 0.7 g fat and 1.3 g fibre.
