Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice)

Nasi goreng (Indonesische gebakken rijst)Asian dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice) (250 g) contains 438 kcal, with 65.5 g of net carbohydrates and 13.8 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 72 (High) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 47.2 (High) for its actual effect.

How many calories are in nasi goreng?

Nasi goreng is one of the best-known street foods in the Netherlands, coming from Indonesia and Suriname. There are 175 calories per 100 g, and a medium plate (250 g) has about 438 calories, with about 14 g of protein and 12 g of fat.

Does nasi goreng raise blood sugar?

Here we have to be honest: white rice is the base of the plate, and its glycemic index is high (about 73). The sweet ketjap sauce adds a little sugar on top, so the calculated index for the plate came out at about 72, which means high.

And more importantly, the glycemic load of a medium plate is about 47, which is very high, because the amount is large. So a full plate causes a clear spike in blood sugar.

How to eat it smartly

  • You can split the plate in two: half rice and half stir-fried vegetables; the load becomes much lighter.
  • Add egg and chicken: protein slows the entry of sugar into the blood.
  • Use less sweet ketjap sauce, and use ordinary soy sauce with a little chilli.
  • Rice cooked the day before and then fried is often gentler on blood sugar than fresh rice, because resistant starch forms in it.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of white rice (73): brown rice (68), or half rice and half riced cauliflower.
  • Instead of a large plate: a small plate with a cucumber salad.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in a plate of nasi goreng?

175 calories per 100 g, and a medium plate (250 g) has about 438 calories.

Does nasi goreng raise blood sugar?

Yes, its calculated index is about 72 (high), and the load of a plate is about 47, because its base is white rice.

Is nasi goreng suitable for weight loss?

It can be, but as a small plate with lots of vegetables and protein, and with less sauce.

What is the difference between nasi goreng and Chinese fried rice?

The idea is the same, but nasi goreng uses sweet ketjap sauce, so it often contains more sugar.
