Liège Waffle with Pearl Sugar

Sweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Liège Waffle with Pearl Sugar (100 g) contains 420 kcal, with 52.2 g of net carbohydrates and 5.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 75 (High) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 39.1 (High) for its actual effect.

Here we have one of the most tempting and richest sweets in Europe! The Liège waffle is different from the light Brussels waffle: it is made from a dense, butter-rich brioche dough studded with large sugar nibs called "pearl sugar" that melt and caramelise on the hot iron into a glassy, crunchy crust. One waffle (100 g) has about 420 kcal, as much as a full meal, and more than 50 g of available carbohydrate, much of it sugar. Our team estimates its glycemic index at about 75 (high), and its glycemic load is very high, so it raises blood sugar quickly. The smart trick: do not deny yourself one on a visit to Belgium, but share it with someone, eat it after a meal with protein and salad rather than on an empty stomach, and walk for 15 to 20 minutes afterwards to help your muscles use up the glucose.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One waffle (100 g, about 420 kcal), ideally half.

Best preparation methods

Share it, eat it after a meal with protein and vegetables, skip extras such as melted chocolate and whipped cream, and walk for 15 to 20 minutes afterwards.

Important Nutritional Note

Its glycemic load is very high, so it is an occasional treat rather than everyday food, and not something for people with diabetes to eat on an empty stomach.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can people with diabetes eat a Liège waffle?

    With great care: half a waffle after a main meal, followed by a walk, is possible on special occasions, but eating it regularly raises your HbA1c.

    Why is a Liège waffle so much heavier than a Brussels waffle?

    Because a Brussels waffle is made from a light, runny batter with whipped egg white, so it turns out airy and crisp, while a Liège waffle is a dense yeast dough rich in butter and pearl sugar.

    Is it a good pre-workout meal?

    For athletes doing long endurance training it can be a quick source of energy to fill glycogen stores; for non-athletes it is a lot of calories that easily exceed needs, and a simpler snack is a better choice.
