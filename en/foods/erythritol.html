What is erythritol?

Want sweetness without sugar? Erythritol is one of the best-known sweeteners you will find in the supermarket, and it is often mixed with stevia. It belongs to the family of "sugar alcohols" and occurs naturally in small amounts in some fruits.

How many calories are in erythritol?

It has almost zero calories. The secret is that the body absorbs it in the gut but does not burn it, and passes it out unchanged. So it makes a "sightseeing trip" through the body and comes back out 😄

Does erythritol raise blood sugar?

No. Its glycemic index is zero, and studies have found that it raises neither blood sugar nor insulin to any real extent. That is why it has become popular on keto and among people watching their blood sugar.

Its taste and how to use it

  • Its taste is close to sugar, but a little lighter (about 70% of its sweetness), and it gives a light cooling sensation on the tongue.
  • You can use it in baking and cooking like sugar, because heat does not spoil it.
  • Many products mix it with stevia so that the sweetness becomes "1:1" like sugar.

Watch out for one thing

Large amounts can cause bloating or diarrhoea, especially if it is mixed with sorbitol. So read the ingredients on the pack and start with small amounts.

Frequently asked questions

Does erythritol raise blood sugar?

No. Its glycemic index is zero, and studies have not found that it raises blood sugar or insulin.

How many calories are in erythritol?

Almost zero.

What is the difference between erythritol and stevia?

Stevia comes from a plant and is hundreds of times sweeter than sugar, so you only need drops of it. Erythritol's sweetness is close to sugar, and you use it in about the same amount.

Is erythritol suitable for keto?

Definitely, because it has almost no calories and does not raise blood sugar.
