Coca Cola

Drinks

Quick answer

A medium portion of Coca Cola (330 g) contains 139 kcal, with 36.3 g of net carbohydrates and 0 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 63 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 22.9 (High) for its actual effect.

The world's best-known soft drink: a refined, quickly absorbed sugar solution with no protein, fibre or nutritional value, and a medium glycemic index (63) that puts heavy demand on insulin.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One can (330 ml) contains about 9 teaspoons of sugar (36 g), which enter the bloodstream within minutes; best avoided altogether if you have diabetes or a fatty liver.

Best preparation methods

Replace it with natural sparkling water with lemon slices and mint leaves, or unsweetened iced tea, to enjoy the refreshment without the sugar.

Important Nutritional Note

Contains caffeine: keep intake moderate, especially in the evening, and ask your doctor if you are pregnant or take medication affected by caffeine.

Frequently asked questions

Is Coca Cola good for weight loss?

Yes. Coca Cola is very low in calories (42 kcal per 100 g), so you can eat a generous amount to add volume and fullness to a meal without many calories.

How does Coca Cola affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 63 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 330 g portion has a glycemic load of 22.9 (High), so it is best to reduce or split the portion and combine it with protein and fibre.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Coca Cola?

A typical 330 g portion of Coca Cola provides about 139 kcal, 0 g protein, 36.3 g carbohydrate, 0 g fat and 0 g fibre.
