How many calories are in tom kha gai?

Tom kha gai is the Thai soup that brings together sour, sweet and creamy: chicken in coconut milk, with lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves and coriander 🍋 There are 145 calories per 100 g, and a bowl (300 g) has about 435 calories, with 33 g of protein and 28 g of fat.

Does tom kha gai raise blood sugar?

No. It has little carbohydrate (about 4 g per 100 g), so the glycemic index does not apply. But if you eat it with a plate of white rice, that is where the effect comes in.

Where are the hidden calories?

Coconut milk! It is what gives the soup its creamy taste, but it is also the source of most of the fat and calories. So the soup is "light" in taste, but not light in calories.

How to eat it smartly

  • At home, you can use light coconut milk, or half coconut milk and half broth.
  • Add more chicken, mushrooms and vegetables.
  • You can eat it on its own as a meal, or with just a quarter plate of rice.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of tom kha (with coconut milk): tom yum, the same sour taste but with a clear broth and far fewer calories.
  • Instead of a full plate of white rice with it: a quarter plate, or none.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in tom kha gai soup?

145 calories per 100 g, and a bowl (300 g) has about 435 calories.

Is tom kha gai suitable for weight loss?

It is high in protein, but coconut milk raises the calories. You can make it lighter with light coconut milk.

What is the difference between tom kha and tom yum?

Tom kha is creamy with coconut milk, while tom yum is sour and spicy with a clear broth and fewer calories.

Does tom kha gai raise blood sugar?

No, it has little carbohydrate. The white rice served with it is what makes the difference.
