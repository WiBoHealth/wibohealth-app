What is doum fruit?

Doum is the fruit of a palm that grows in Upper Egypt and Sudan. It is brown with a very hard shell, and its taste has a light sweetness reminiscent of gingerbread. The best-known way to have it is doum drink: the powder is soaked in water and drunk cold.

How many calories are in doum?

There are 125 calories and 14 g of fibre per 100 g of doum! That is a very high figure, which makes doum one of the fruits richest in fibre. A portion (30 g) has about 38 calories and 4 g of fibre.

Does doum raise blood sugar?

Here we have to be honest with you: there is no published measurement of the glycemic index of doum in humans, so we will not give you a number of our own.

What we know is that it has about 12 g of available carbohydrate per 100 g, and the high fibre usually slows absorption. There are studies on doum extracts in animals, but that does not mean the same result in humans.

Doum and blood pressure

Many people search for "does doum lower blood pressure". So far there are no human studies that confirm this, so doum is a lovely traditional food, not a treatment.

How to eat it smartly

  • If you are making doum drink, watch the sugar added to it, because it is often heavily sweetened.
  • You can drink it without sugar or with two drops of stevia.
  • If you chew dried pieces of doum, watch your teeth, because it is very hard!
