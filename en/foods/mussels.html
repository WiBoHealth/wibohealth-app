Zeeland Steamed Mussels

Zeeuwse mosselen gekooktMeat, fish & eggs

Quick answer

A medium portion of Zeeland Steamed Mussels (150 g) contains 129 kcal, with 5.6 g of net carbohydrates and 17.9 g of protein.

Mussels are among the most popular and beneficial seafoods: attractive orange meat with a rich, much-loved taste, offering a nourishing meal at a sensible price. Their glycemic index does not apply, and they are a true mine of iron that fights anaemia and chronic fatigue.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A famous and healthy dish is mussels cooked in a tomato, garlic and herb broth; spoon up the broth and enjoy the fullness without needing white bread.

Best preparation methods

Cook them for a few minutes in a covered pot with onion, chopped garlic, coriander and lemon juice, and discard straight away any mussel that does not open during cooking.

Important Nutritional Note

They contain exceptional amounts of vitamin B12 and iron and a little more glycogen than most seafood, so their carbohydrate counts as about 4 g per 100 g, which is a very small amount.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do mussels help with iron deficiency and anaemia?

    Very much. They contain well-absorbed haem iron in amounts comparable to red meat, with far fewer calories and less fat.

    What is the nutritional value of a 150 g portion of cooked mussels?

    It gives you about 130 kcal, 18 g of high-quality protein and more than 300% of your daily vitamin B12 needs.

    Do mussels raise blood sugar in people with diabetes?

    They have no real effect on blood sugar thanks to their low natural starch and their high, filling protein content.
