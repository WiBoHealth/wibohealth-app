Dutch Mini Pancakes (Poffertjes)

Sweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Dutch Mini Pancakes (Poffertjes) (50 g) contains 140 kcal, with 20.2 g of net carbohydrates and 2.8 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 13.8 (Medium) for its actual effect.

The charming little sponge pancakes that Dutch winter stalls are famous for: made from buckwheat and wheat flour with yeast, baked in special copper moulds and served swimming in butter and icing sugar. They have a high glycemic index (≈68) and deliver fast carbohydrate energy.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A dish for dessert and for sharing with family on outings; stop at 4 to 5 small poffertjes and avoid eating the whole plate on your own to prevent sharp sugar spikes and sluggishness.

Best preparation methods

Ask for them with just a light brush of real butter and keep the icing sugar on top to a minimum; you can garnish them with a few fresh berries to add useful fibre.

Important Nutritional Note

The buckwheat in the batter adds some minerals and antioxidants, but the icing sugar and excess butter make this a high-calorie dish that calls for strict moderation.
