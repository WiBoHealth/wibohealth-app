How many calories are in yerba mate?

In Syria and Lebanon, mate has become part of sitting together with friends even more than in Argentina itself 😄 And the good news: a cup of mate (240 ml) without sugar has only about 7 calories. So you can drink several cups while you sit together without thinking about calories.

Does yerba mate raise blood sugar?

Mate on its own does not raise blood sugar, because it has half a gram of carbohydrate per 100 ml, so the glycemic index does not apply to it.

But like every drink, the story is the sugar you add on top. And since mate is refilled several times in the same cup, the spoonful of sugar with every refill adds up fast!

Yerba mate and caffeine

Mate contains caffeine, and that is what gives it its feeling of energy. So if you drink a lot of it in the evenings, it can affect your sleep. You can have your last cup a few hours before bed.

How to drink it smartly

  • You can drink it without sugar from the start; you get used to the light bitter taste quickly.
  • If you want some sweetness, you can try a drop of stevia in the cup.
  • Watch out for water that is too hot: it is best hot but not boiling, because that way the taste comes out smoother.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of mate with sugar: mate without sugar, or with a drop of stevia.
  • Instead of sweet coffee in the evening: a light cup of mate without sugar.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in a cup of yerba mate?

About 7 calories per cup (240 ml), if it is without sugar.

Does yerba mate raise blood sugar?

On its own, no. The sugar you add with every refill is what makes the difference.

Does yerba mate contain caffeine?

Yes, and that is why it gives a feeling of energy. Cut down in the evening if it affects your sleep.

Does yerba mate help with weight loss?

It is a drink with almost no calories, so it works well on a diet. But no drink burns fat on its own.
