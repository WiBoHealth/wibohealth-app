Dutch Apple Pie (Appeltaart)

Sweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Dutch Apple Pie (Appeltaart) (100 g) contains 285 kcal, with 42.2 g of net carbohydrates and 3.2 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 41 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 17.3 (Medium) for its actual effect.

How many calories are in apple pie?

Appeltaart is the pride of Dutch cafés, and no visit to the Netherlands is complete without a slice with cream! There are 285 calories per 100 g, and a slice (about 100 g) has about the same, with about 10 g of fat. And if you put whipped cream on top, add even more.

Does apple pie raise blood sugar?

Here is a small surprise: baked apple pie was measured in an Australian study, and its glycemic index came out at 41, which means low. The reason is that apples themselves have a low index (36), and the fat in the pastry slows digestion.

But as with luqaimat, a low index is not the whole story. The glycemic load of a slice is about 17, which means medium, and the calories are high. Its insulin index is 47, which means it stimulates insulin moderately.

How to eat it smartly

  • One slice with a cup of coffee is enough.
  • Order it without cream, or with just a small spoonful.
  • You can eat it after a meal, not as a meal on its own.
  • At home, you can cut the sugar in the recipe by a third and add more apple and cinnamon. The taste stays sweet.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of a slice of appeltaart with cream: a slice without cream, or half a slice.
  • Instead of the whole pie: an oven-baked apple with cinnamon and a spoon of Greek yoghurt.
