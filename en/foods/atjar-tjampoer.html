Indonesian Atjar Tjampoer Sweet Pickled Veg with Turmeric

Sauces, pickles & olives

Quick answer

A medium portion of Indonesian Atjar Tjampoer Sweet Pickled Veg with Turmeric (50 g) contains 26 kcal, with 4.6 g of net carbohydrates and 0.4 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 45 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 2.1 (Low) for its actual effect.

Serving advice

Two spoonfuls next to a rich meal cut through the heaviness, and the sourness brings freshness to the plate.

Best ways to prepare it

Make it at home by soaking cabbage and carrot in vinegar with turmeric and mustard seeds, with a pinch of stevia instead of sugar.

Important nutrition note ⚠️

Drain off the sugary pickling liquid before serving to reduce the free sugar you take in.

Frequently asked questions

Is atjar tjampoer suitable for someone with diabetes?

Yes, in a moderate portion (50 g gives only about 4.5 g of carbs), and the natural acetic acid in it may help slow stomach emptying.

Is it a source of live probiotics?

The jars in the supermarket are heat-pasteurised and pickled in vinegar, so they contain no live bacteria. But the vegetable fibre feeds the good gut bacteria.

How do I find sugar-free pickled vegetables in the shop?

Read the ingredients and look for jars made without sugar or added glucose syrup. Try the Boodi app 💚 to check a product's ingredients and find lower-sugar alternatives.
