Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free)

Alcoholvrije piña coladaDrinks

Quick answer

A medium portion of Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free) (200 g) contains 230 kcal, with 34.4 g of net carbohydrates and 2.4 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 58 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 20 (High) for its actual effect.

Tropical islands in a creamy glass: fresh golden pineapple juice blended with coconut milk and crushed ice. A rich, velvety drink combining the tang of pineapple with the sweetness of coconut, without a drop of alcohol.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Fresh pineapple contains bromelain, an enzyme that breaks down proteins, though the amount in juice is limited.

Best preparation methods

Coconut milk is rich in saturated fat and calories, so treat a glass as a liquid dessert rather than an everyday drink.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free) good for weight loss?

    It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 200 g portion gives 230 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

    How does Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free) affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 58 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 200 g portion has a glycemic load of 20 (High), so it is best to reduce or split the portion and combine it with protein and fibre.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free)?

    A typical 200 g portion of Virgin Piña Colada (Alcohol-Free) provides about 230 kcal, 2.4 g protein, 36 g carbohydrate, 9 g fat and 1.6 g fibre.

    How many calories are in a virgin piña colada?

    A medium 200ml glass provides about 230 kcal and 36g of carbohydrates from the pineapple and coconut milk.
