Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek)

Gevulde koek met amandelspijsSweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek) (50 g) contains 220 kcal, with 25.6 g of net carbohydrates and 3.4 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 17.4 (Medium) for its actual effect.

Gevulde koek is one of the best-loved traditional bakes in the Netherlands: a glossy golden round of crumbly butter pastry, topped with an almond in the centre and hiding a dense almond paste filling (amandelspijs). It combines crisp edges with a soft almond heart. Nutritionally, though, it is a festive treat high in calories and carbohydrate: a whole cake (about 100g) gives around 440 kcal and 30g of sugar, so it deserves mindful enjoyment and a measured portion to avoid blood sugar spikes.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A sensible portion is half a cake (about 50g), eaten slowly with a cup of black coffee or unsweetened green tea.

Best preparation methods

Avoid eating it on an empty stomach in the morning: as a dessert after a lunch rich in fibre and protein, its sugar is absorbed more slowly and the afternoon energy dip is smaller.

Important Nutritional Note

The original cake contains wheat flour, egg, butter and almonds (several allergens). Some commercial bakeries replace almond paste with a cheaper filling called banketspijs, often made from legumes or soy with almond flavouring, so read the ingredient list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek) good for weight loss?

    Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek) provides 440 kcal per 100 g, so it is high in calories and fits a weight-loss plan only in small, measured amounts; a 50 g portion gives 220 kcal.

    How does Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek) affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 50 g portion has a glycemic load of 17.4 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek)?

    A typical 50 g portion of Dutch Almond Paste Stuffed Cake (Gevulde Koek) provides about 220 kcal, 3.4 g protein, 27 g carbohydrate, 11 g fat and 1.4 g fibre.

    What is the glycemic index of gevulde koek?

    Its glycemic index is medium (68), and the glycemic load of half a cake (50g) is about 17.4, medium but close to high. People with diabetes or insulin resistance should count it as a concentrated sugar portion and eat it with care.

    Are the fats in gevulde koek healthy or harmful?

    It is a mix: the almond fat is beneficial monounsaturated fat, while the pastry contains butter, which is high in saturated fat. Eat it in moderation.

    How can I tell whether the cake has real almond filling?

    Look for “amandelspijs” (almond paste) in the ingredient list and avoid packs that say “banketspijs”, a substitute filling usually based on legumes or soy with almond flavouring.
