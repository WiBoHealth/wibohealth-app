Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread

Braziliaanse pão de queijo tapioca kaasbroodjesLatin American dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread (100 g) contains 275 kcal, with 33.8 g of net carbohydrates and 6 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 23 (High) for its actual effect.

Famous golden Brazilian cheese breads with a chewy texture and melting cheese, made from natural tapioca starch and Minas cheese; naturally gluten-free, but portions need watching because tapioca starch breaks down quickly.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A great choice if you avoid gluten, but tapioca starch is absorbed quickly, so two pieces are enough as a snack.

Best preparation methods

Eat them warm straight from the oven to enjoy the soft cheese without adding butter.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread good for weight loss?

    It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 100 g portion gives 275 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

    How does Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 68 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 100 g portion has a glycemic load of 23 (High), so it is best to reduce or split the portion and combine it with protein and fibre.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread?

    A typical 100 g portion of Brazilian Pão de Queijo Tapioca Cheese Bread provides about 275 kcal, 6 g protein, 35 g carbohydrate, 12.5 g fat and 1.2 g fibre.
