Quick answer

A medium portion of Whole Barley Flakes with Beta-Glucan (40 g) contains 134 kcal, with 21.6 g of net carbohydrates and 4.2 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 28 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 6 (Low) for its actual effect.

The traditional grain of talbina and one of the steadiest grains for blood sugar: lightly hulled barley flakes pressed with their bran, rich in soluble beta-glucan fibre. A firm, satisfying crunch that makes them an excellent choice for a healthy breakfast.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Whole barley is among the lowest-GI grains (GI = 28), which makes it an excellent choice for people with diabetes and anyone looking for long-lasting fullness.

Best preparation methods

Under EU rules: barley beta-glucans contribute to the maintenance of normal blood cholesterol levels; the beneficial effect is obtained with a daily intake of 3g.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Whole Barley Flakes with Beta-Glucan good for weight loss?

    It can be included in moderation: it contains 14 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 40 g portion gives 134 kcal.

    How does Whole Barley Flakes with Beta-Glucan affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 28 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 40 g portion has a glycemic load of 6 (Low), so the effect of this portion on blood sugar is limited.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Whole Barley Flakes with Beta-Glucan?

    A typical 40 g portion of Whole Barley Flakes with Beta-Glucan provides about 134 kcal, 4.2 g protein, 27.2 g carbohydrate, 0.9 g fat and 5.6 g fibre.

    Are barley flakes suitable for gluten intolerance?

    No. Barley contains a natural gluten protein (hordein), so it is not suitable for people with coeliac disease or gluten intolerance.
