Broccoli

Gestoomde broccoliVegetables

Quick answer

A medium portion of Broccoli (90 g) contains 31 kcal, with 4 g of net carbohydrates and 2.5 g of protein.

The giant of the cruciferous vegetable family and a source of sulforaphane, a compound with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Its dense fibre reins in sugar absorption and feeds beneficial gut bacteria very efficiently.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Eat a small plate of it at the start of lunch; its fibre forms a natural barrier in the gut that helps prevent sudden spikes in blood sugar after the meal.

Best preparation methods

Steam it for just 4 to 5 minutes so it keeps its bright green colour and pleasant crunch, and avoid long boiling in water so it does not lose its vitamin C and active compounds.

Important Nutritional Note

This food does not need a glycemic index, but the foods eaten with it determine the meal’s effect on blood sugar.

Frequently asked questions

Is Broccoli good for weight loss?

Yes. Broccoli is very low in calories (34 kcal per 100 g), so you can eat a generous amount to add volume and fullness to a meal without many calories.

How does Broccoli affect blood sugar?

Broccoli contains only 4.4 g of available carbohydrate per 100 g, so the glycemic index does not apply and its direct effect on blood sugar is minimal. The meal’s effect depends on what you eat with it, such as bread, rice or sweetened sauces.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Broccoli?

A typical 90 g portion of Broccoli provides about 31 kcal, 2.5 g protein, 6.3 g carbohydrate, 0.4 g fat and 2.3 g fibre.
