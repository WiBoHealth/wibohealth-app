Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks

Brusselse stoemp met wortel en preiSoups & home-cooked dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks (180 g) contains 207 kcal, with 27 g of net carbohydrates and 4 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 60 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 16.2 (Medium) for its actual effect.

Stoemp is the comfort food of Brussels and Belgium: mashed potato mixed with a large amount of boiled vegetables and butter, especially leeks, carrots and cabbage. It is smarter than plain mashed potato, because the vegetables dilute the starch in every bite and add fibre, and the butter slows digestion a little. Our team estimates its glycemic index at about 60 (medium), compared with a high GI for mashed potato on its own. A large portion still pushes up the glycemic load (16.2 for 180 g, which is medium), so eat it alongside grilled meat or fish and a salad rather than as the main part of the meal.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A moderate portion (180 g, about 207 kcal).

Best preparation methods

Use more vegetables (leek, carrot, cauliflower) than potato, boil the potatoes in their skins and let them cool before mashing, and serve with grilled meat or fish and a green salad.

Important Nutritional Note

Its glycemic load is medium, so people with diabetes should not go over the portion and should skip extras such as fried sausages or rich gravy.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks good for weight loss?

    It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 180 g portion gives 207 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

    How does Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 60 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 180 g portion has a glycemic load of 16.2 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks?

    A typical 180 g portion of Brussels Stoemp with Carrots & Leeks provides about 207 kcal, 4 g protein, 32 g carbohydrate, 8.1 g fat and 5 g fibre.

    How can I eat Belgian stoemp safely if I have insulin resistance?

    Make it about 70% vegetables (leek, cauliflower, carrot) and only 30% potato, and boil the potatoes, then chill them in the fridge before mashing and reheating, so some "resistant starch" forms that slightly softens the rise in blood sugar.

    Is it good for children and athletes?

    Yes, it is a good energy and nutrient meal, with beta-carotene from the carrots and vitamin C and potassium from the potatoes and vegetables, and it is easy to digest and popular.

    Is milk added?

    The traditional recipe uses the vegetable cooking water, butter, nutmeg and a little milk, and lactose-free milk works just as well.
