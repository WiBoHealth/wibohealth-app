How many calories are in shirataki?

Shirataki is a Japanese "pasta" made from the root of a plant called konjac. There are only 9 calories per 100 g, and a bag (200 g) has about 18 calories. For comparison, a plate of ordinary pasta has hundreds of calories!

Does shirataki raise blood sugar?

No. It is mostly water and a fibre called glucomannan, and it has almost zero available carbohydrate, so the glycemic index does not apply to it. That is why it has become popular on keto and weight-loss diets.

What to know before you try it

  • The smell: when you first open the bag there is a light "fishy" smell. Don't worry, it goes away when you rinse it well with water.
  • The texture: a bit rubbery, not like ordinary pasta.
  • The taste: it has no taste of its own, so it takes on the taste of the sauce you cook it in.
  • Fullness: it fills the stomach, but it has no protein, so you need to add chicken, eggs or tofu.

How to cook it

  1. Rinse it well under running water for a full minute.
  2. You can boil it for two minutes.
  3. Most important: dry-fry it in a pan without oil for a few minutes until it dries out; that makes it much tastier.
  4. Then add it to the sauce, vegetables and protein.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of a plate of white pasta: half pasta and half shirataki; it cuts the calories without losing the taste.
  • Instead of instant ramen noodles: shirataki with broth and vegetables.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in shirataki noodles?

9 calories per 100 g, and a bag (200 g) has about 18 calories.

Does shirataki raise blood sugar?

No, it has almost zero available carbohydrate.

Is shirataki suitable for keto and weight loss?

Definitely, because it has almost no calories and no starch. But you need to add protein to feel full.

Why does shirataki smell?

From the water it is stored in. Rinse it well and dry-fry it in a pan, and the smell goes away.
