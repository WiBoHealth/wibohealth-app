Indonesian Soto Ayam Chicken Soup

Asian dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Indonesian Soto Ayam Chicken Soup (350 g) contains 438 kcal, with 41.3 g of net carbohydrates and 42 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 45 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 18.6 (Medium) for its actual effect.

Serving advice

One bowl is a complete lunch or dinner. Use less rice vermicelli (bihun) and more bean sprouts (taugé) and cabbage to cut the carbs by half.

Best ways to prepare it

Squeeze half a lime over the hot broth and add a dash of fresh hot sambal: citrus and chilli add a fresh flavour, so you need less salt.

Important nutrition note ⚠️

Turmeric is what gives the broth its golden colour, thanks to its natural pigment curcumin. Just watch the salt level if you have high blood pressure.

Frequently asked questions

Is soto ayam soup great for dieting and building muscle?

Yes, very much so: a bowl gives you 42 g of high-quality protein with moderate fat (under 10 g). Protein contributes to the growth and maintenance of muscle mass, and it keeps you full for a long time without empty calories.

How can I lower the glycemic load of soto if I have diabetes?

The key is the noodles and rice: swap the rice noodles for shirataki (konjac) or double the cabbage and crunchy bean sprouts, and the glycemic load of the bowl drops clearly.

How do I choose healthy ingredients for ready-made soto from a toko?

Avoid ready-made spice pastes high in salt and added sugar, and choose a paste with simple ingredients. Try the Boodi app 💚 to check a product's ingredients and find lower-sugar alternatives.
