Gulf Balaleet (Sweet Vermicelli with Egg)

Balaleet uit de Golf (zoete vermicelli met ei)Arabic dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Gulf Balaleet (Sweet Vermicelli with Egg) (144 g) contains 353 kcal, with 53 g of net carbohydrates and 11.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 63 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 33.4 (High) for its actual effect.

How many calories are in balaleet?

Balaleet is the breakfast of Eid and morning gatherings in the Gulf: vermicelli cooked with sugar, ghee, cardamom and saffron, topped with a fried omelette. Sweet and savoury on the same plate! There are 245 calories per 100 g, and the typical plate (144 g) has about 353 calories.

Does balaleet raise blood sugar?

The good news is that balaleet has actually been measured in an Emirati study, and its glycemic index came out at 63, which means medium. The egg on top plays a part, because protein slows absorption.

But in terms of amount, the glycemic load of a plate is about 33, which means high, because it is based on white vermicelli and sugar.

How to eat it smartly

  • Cut the sugar in the recipe by half: cardamom and saffron give a strong flavour, so you won't notice much difference.
  • Add more egg: two eggs instead of one make the plate more filling and gentler on blood sugar.
  • A small plate is enough, especially with dates and coffee.
  • You can use just one spoon of ghee instead of a large amount.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of a big plate of balaleet: a small plate with two eggs.
  • If you want a lighter Gulf breakfast: harees (42 in the same study, which means low).

Frequently asked questions
