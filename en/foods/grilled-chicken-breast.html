Grilled Chicken Breast

Gegrilde kipfiletMeat, fish & eggs

Quick answer

A medium portion of Grilled Chicken Breast (100 g) contains 165 kcal, with 0 g of net carbohydrates and 31 g of protein.

The purest source of lean, carbohydrate-free protein. It supports building muscle mass and increases daily energy expenditure, without causing any rebound or rise in glucose levels.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

The essential companion for lunch or dinner; eat it first, before any starch on your plate, to help put the brakes on the absorption of the whole meal.

Best preparation methods

Season it with olive oil, lemon and natural herbs (such as thyme and rosemary) and grill it in the oven or a dry pan; avoid breaded frying and hydrogenated oils.

Important Nutritional Note

This food does not need a glycemic index, but the foods eaten with it determine the meal’s effect on blood sugar.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grilled Chicken Breast good for weight loss?

It can be a useful part of a weight-loss plan because it provides 18.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, and protein supports fullness. A 100 g portion gives 165 kcal.

How does Grilled Chicken Breast affect blood sugar?

Grilled Chicken Breast contains virtually no available carbohydrate, so the glycemic index does not apply and its direct effect on blood sugar is minimal. The meal’s effect depends on what you eat with it, such as bread, rice or sweetened sauces.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Grilled Chicken Breast?

A typical 100 g portion of Grilled Chicken Breast provides about 165 kcal, 31 g protein, 0 g carbohydrate, 3.6 g fat and 0 g fibre.
