Coconut Sugar

KokossuikerSpecial ingredients & supplements

Quick answer

A medium portion of Coconut Sugar (4 g) contains 15 kcal, with 3.6 g of net carbohydrates and 0 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 54 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 1.9 (Low) for its actual effect.

How many calories are in coconut sugar?

Coconut sugar is made from the sap of the coconut palm's flower, boiled until it becomes brown granules. There are 375 calories per 100 g, and a teaspoon (4 g) has about 15 calories, which is about the same as white sugar (16 calories per spoon).

Does coconut sugar raise blood sugar?

Here there is a story we need to correct: many packs say "low glycemic index". The truth is that the University of Sydney measured it and found an index of 54, which is right on the edge of low. Another measurement from the Philippines gave 35. White sugar is about 65.

So it is a little gentler than white sugar, but the difference is not as big as the marketing suggests. In the end, the body handles it as sugar.

Is it healthier than white sugar?

It contains small amounts of minerals, but in the amount you use (a spoon or two) the difference is negligible. So if you choose it "because it is healthy", the amount is what makes the difference, not the type of sugar.

How to use it smartly

  • You can use it in baking and cooking instead of brown sugar; its taste has a nice touch of caramel.
  • Cut the amount by half in any recipe, and you probably won't notice the difference.
  • If you want sweetness without any sugar at all, go for stevia or erythritol.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in coconut sugar?

375 calories per 100 g, and about 15 calories per teaspoon, about the same as white sugar.

Does coconut sugar raise blood sugar?

Yes, but a little more gently than white sugar. Its index is 54 according to the University of Sydney.

Is coconut sugar suitable for people with diabetes?

In the end it is sugar, so it raises blood sugar. The amount matters most, and calorie-free sweeteners such as stevia are a better choice.

Is coconut sugar healthier than ordinary sugar?

It contains minerals in small amounts, but in the amount you eat the difference is negligible.
