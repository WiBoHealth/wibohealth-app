The famous Mexican hot green pepper with a sharp, herby flavour and a pleasant medium heat. It is eaten in small, gram-sized amounts, its carbohydrate is negligible, so a glycemic index does not apply, and it is known for capsaicin, which stimulates metabolism.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Add fresh jalapeño slices to tacos, salads and egg dishes; the heat wakes up your senses, increases satiety hormones and reduces the urge to load up on starch.

Best preparation methods

Remove the seeds and white inner ribs if you want less heat and more of the herby flavour, and avoid commercial canned jalapeños steeped in sugar and preservatives.

Important Nutritional Note

The capsaicin in jalapeño supports circulation and your natural energy burn; if you have a sensitive stomach or reflux, strict moderation keeps you comfortable and avoids irritation.
