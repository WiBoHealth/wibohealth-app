Tortilla Wrap

Tortilla wrapBreads & pastries

Quick answer

A medium portion of Tortilla Wrap (60 g) contains 131 kcal, with 20.2 g of net carbohydrates and 3.4 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 52 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 10.5 (Medium) for its actual effect.

The flat, flexible bread at the heart of the best-known Latin dishes such as tacos and fajitas. Traditional corn tortillas have a low glycemic index (≈52) thanks to the lime treatment of the corn (nixtamalisation), which preserves its fibre, while white wheat tortillas are absorbed faster and are higher in calories.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Always choose yellow corn or wholegrain wheat tortillas and keep to two small ones, filled generously with protein such as chicken or fish, leafy greens and avocado.

Best preparation methods

Warm tortillas in a hot, dry pan without oil; dry heating restores their softness and natural toasted flavour without adding hidden fat calories.

Important Nutritional Note

Check the ingredient label on packaged tortillas, as many commercial products add hydrogenated vegetable oils and sugars to keep them flexible for longer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tortilla Wrap good for weight loss?

It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 60 g portion gives 131 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

How does Tortilla Wrap affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 52 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 60 g portion has a glycemic load of 10.5 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Tortilla Wrap?

A typical 60 g portion of Tortilla Wrap provides about 131 kcal, 3.4 g protein, 21.6 g carbohydrate, 3.4 g fat and 1.4 g fibre.
