Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes

Russische boekweitblini mini pannenkoekjesGrains & starches

Quick answer

A medium portion of Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes (100 g) contains 195 kcal, with 29.2 g of net carbohydrates and 6.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 50 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 14.6 (Medium) for its actual effect.

Small, spongy Russian pancakes made from nutritious buckwheat flour; a gently earthy taste, rich in the antioxidant rutin and fibre, with a low glycemic index (50). The values on this page are for plain blini without toppings.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Buckwheat flour is naturally gluten-free, but many blini recipes mix it with wheat flour, so check the ingredients if you avoid gluten.

Best preparation methods

Serve them with smoked salmon and cottage cheese for a breakfast rich in protein and healthy fats instead of sugary jam.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes good for weight loss?

    It can fit a balanced plan with portion control: a 100 g portion gives 195 kcal, and what matters most is your total daily intake and the rest of the meal.

    How does Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 50 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 100 g portion has a glycemic load of 14.6 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes?

    A typical 100 g portion of Russian Buckwheat Blini Mini Pancakes provides about 195 kcal, 6.5 g protein, 32 g carbohydrate, 4.8 g fat and 2.8 g fibre.
