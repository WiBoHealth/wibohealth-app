How many calories are in linden tea?

The smell of linden in winter reminds you of your grandmother's house 😌 Dried linden tree flowers, steeped in hot water. A cup (240 ml) without sugar has only about 5 calories, so practically nothing.

Does linden tea raise blood sugar?

Without sugar, no. It has almost no carbohydrate, so the glycemic index does not apply. The sugar or honey you add is what makes the difference.

Linden and colds

Many people drink linden when they have a cold or the flu, and feel that it soothes them. The truth is that the hot drink itself moistens the throat and warms you up, and that is comforting. But there are not enough studies proving that linden treats colds, so it is a warm and gentle drink, not a medicine.

How to make it

  • One heaped spoon of flowers in a cup of hot (not boiling) water, covered, for 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Don't boil it on the stove, because it loses its aroma.
  • If you want sweetness, two drops of stevia or half a spoon of honey.
  • You can mix it with lemon peel or a cinnamon stick.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of a cup of tea with two spoons of sugar: a cup of linden without sugar.
  • Instead of sweetened hot drinks in the evening: linden, because it has no caffeine.

Frequently asked questions

About 5 calories per cup (240 ml), without sugar.

Does linden tea contain caffeine?

No, linden has no caffeine, so it is fine in the evening.

Does linden tea cure a cold?

The hot drink soothes the throat, but there are not enough studies showing that it treats colds. It is a gentle drink, not a medicine.

How do I make linden tea properly?
