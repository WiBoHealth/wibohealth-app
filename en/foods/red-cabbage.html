Red Cabbage

Rode koolVegetables

Quick answer

A medium portion of Red Cabbage (80 g) contains 25 kcal, with 4.2 g of net carbohydrates and 1.1 g of protein.

A cheerful deep-red colour and a store of antioxidants on the salad table: crisp cabbage leaves full of anthocyanins and vitamin C. Its net carbohydrate is very low and the glycemic index does not apply; one of the best vegetables for supporting healthy blood vessels and arteries.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Make grated red cabbage a daily part of your green salads; its dense crunch needs a lot of chewing, which encourages early fullness signals in the brain.

Best preparation methods

Massage the shredded cabbage by hand with a little sea salt, apple cider vinegar and olive oil 10 minutes before serving; this softens the tough fibre and makes it easier to digest.

Important Nutritional Note

Rich in vitamin K and natural sulphur compounds that are good for the stomach; an exceptional choice for people with diabetes and anyone focused on metabolic health, with no real limit on the amount.

Frequently asked questions

Is Red Cabbage good for weight loss?

Yes. Red Cabbage is very low in calories (31 kcal per 100 g), so you can eat a generous amount to add volume and fullness to a meal without many calories.

How does Red Cabbage affect blood sugar?

Red Cabbage contains only 5.3 g of available carbohydrate per 100 g, so the glycemic index does not apply and its direct effect on blood sugar is minimal. The meal’s effect depends on what you eat with it, such as bread, rice or sweetened sauces.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Red Cabbage?

A typical 80 g portion of Red Cabbage provides about 25 kcal, 1.1 g protein, 5.9 g carbohydrate, 0.2 g fat and 1.7 g fibre.
