Serving advice

Ideal as a replacement for ketchup and sweetened commercial sauces full of fructose syrup.

Best ways to prepare it

Add half a teaspoon to season meat, eggs or soups for a concentrated flavour that makes the meal more satisfying.

Important nutrition note ⚠️

Avoid it if you have acid reflux, an inflamed stomach or an active irritable bowel, because it is very hot.

Frequently asked questions

Does sambal oelek have any effect on blood sugar?

It doesn't raise blood sugar at all: its glycemic index and load are close to zero because it contains no added sugar.

Does hot chili help with weight control?

It adds strong flavour with almost no calories, so it can replace sweet sauces. The effect of capsaicin on calorie burning is small, though, and not something to rely on for weight loss.

How can I be sure the sambal in the shop is genuine and sugar-free?

Some brands add starch and sugar to tone down the heat. Read the ingredients: the real thing is just chili, salt and vinegar. Try the Boodi app 💚 to check a product's ingredients and find lower-sugar alternatives.
