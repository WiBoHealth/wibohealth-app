Butter Croissant

Boter croissantSweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Butter Croissant (60 g) contains 244 kcal, with 26 g of net carbohydrates and 4.8 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 67 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 17.4 (Medium) for its actual effect.

The smell of a fresh French butter croissant in the morning is irresistible and a much-loved start to the day, but the most common question is: does it raise blood sugar quickly? The answer is yes; the mix of refined white flour and layers of butter makes its carbohydrate absorbed relatively fast, with a glycemic index of 67, which can give instant energy followed by a quick dip and early hunger.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Don't make a croissant your only breakfast on an empty stomach; always pair it with a filling protein such as a boiled egg or a slice of natural cheese, and swap juices and sweetened drinks for black coffee or warm tea without sugar.

Best preparation methods

Warming the croissant in the oven for a few minutes brings out the natural butter flavour and helps you be satisfied with half or one small croissant, eaten slowly and with enjoyment rather than in a rush.

Important Nutritional Note

If you watch your blood sugar or are managing your weight, enjoy croissants as a planned weekly exception and focus on protein for steady fullness all morning.

Frequently asked questions

Is Butter Croissant good for weight loss?

Butter Croissant provides 406 kcal per 100 g, so it is high in calories and fits a weight-loss plan only in small, measured amounts; a 60 g portion gives 244 kcal.

How does Butter Croissant affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 67 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 60 g portion has a glycemic load of 17.4 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Butter Croissant?

A typical 60 g portion of Butter Croissant provides about 244 kcal, 4.8 g protein, 27.6 g carbohydrate, 12.6 g fat and 1.6 g fibre.
