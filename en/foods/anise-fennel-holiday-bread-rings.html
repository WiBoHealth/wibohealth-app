Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds

Levantijnse feestkaak met anijs en zwarte komijnBreads & pastries

Quick answer

A medium portion of Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds (30 g) contains 110 kcal, with 17.3 g of net carbohydrates and 2.3 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 55 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 9.5 (Low) for its actual effect.

The bread rings of memories and blessed seasons, baked until golden and crisp; the dough holds a traditional spice blend (anise, fennel, mahlab and nigella seeds) kneaded with olive oil and ghee, filling the house with the scent of the holidays.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Anise and fennel are traditionally added to kaak dough for their flavour and their reputation for settling the stomach after starchy food.

Best preparation methods

A good alternative to syrup-soaked sweets; one ring in the morning with tea or labneh is enough.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds good for weight loss?

    It can be included in moderation: it contains 4.2 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 30 g portion gives 110 kcal.

    How does Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 55 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 30 g portion has a glycemic load of 9.5 (Low), so the effect of this portion on blood sugar is limited.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds?

    A typical 30 g portion of Levantine Eid Kaak with Anise & Nigella Seeds provides about 110 kcal, 2.3 g protein, 18.6 g carbohydrate, 2.9 g fat and 1.3 g fibre.

    Is herb Eid kaak high in added sugar?

    Its sweetness is mild compared with maamoul and ghorayeba, as it relies more on the flavour of anise and nigella seeds.
