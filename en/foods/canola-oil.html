Canola (Rapeseed) Oil

Koolzaadolie (canola)Healthy fats

A medium portion of Canola (Rapeseed) Oil (10 g) contains 88 kcal, with 0 g of net carbohydrates and 0 g of protein.

One of the most widely used everyday cooking oils, pressed from rapeseed improved by conventional breeding; it is very low in saturated fat compared with other vegetable oils.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

An affordable choice for baking and frying thanks to its neutral taste and moderate heat resistance.

Best preparation methods

Avoid reusing the oil for repeated frying, so that harmful oxidised compounds do not build up.

Important Nutritional Note

This food does not need a glycemic index, but the foods eaten with it determine the meal’s effect on blood sugar.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Canola (Rapeseed) Oil good for weight loss?

    Canola (Rapeseed) Oil provides 884 kcal per 100 g, so it is high in calories and fits a weight-loss plan only in small, measured amounts; a 10 g portion gives 88 kcal.

    How does Canola (Rapeseed) Oil affect blood sugar?

    Canola (Rapeseed) Oil contains virtually no available carbohydrate, so the glycemic index does not apply and its direct effect on blood sugar is minimal. The meal’s effect depends on what you eat with it, such as bread, rice or sweetened sauces.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Canola (Rapeseed) Oil?

    A typical 10 g portion of Canola (Rapeseed) Oil provides about 88 kcal, 0 g protein, 0 g carbohydrate, 10 g fat and 0 g fibre.

    Is canola oil suitable for weight-loss diets?

    Like any pure oil it is a concentrated source of energy (about 88 kcal per 10g), so keeping portions moderate is what matters for weight control.
