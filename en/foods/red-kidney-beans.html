Red Kidney Beans

Rode kidneybonenLegumes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Red Kidney Beans (150 g) contains 190 kcal, with 23.4 g of net carbohydrates and 13 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 37 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 8.7 (Low) for its actual effect.

Rich in powerful antioxidants and soluble fibre, with a low glycemic index (37) that supports healthy gut bacteria and keeps the blood sugar line flat and stable.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Excellent in salads, soups and stews; their protective network of viscous fibre helps keep post-meal blood sugar readings in check.

Best preparation methods

An important safety rule: if you use dried beans, soak them well and then boil them vigorously in water for at least 10 minutes to fully destroy the toxic lectin protein before you continue cooking.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.

Frequently asked questions

Are Red Kidney Beans good for weight loss?

It can be included in moderation: it contains 7.4 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 150 g portion gives 191 kcal.

How do Red Kidney Beans affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 37 (Low) according to Atkinson 2021. A typical 150 g portion has a glycemic load of 8.7 (Low), so the effect of this portion on blood sugar is limited.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Red Kidney Beans?

A typical 150 g portion of Red Kidney Beans provides about 191 kcal, 13 g protein, 34.5 g carbohydrate, 0.8 g fat and 11.1 g fibre.
