Bami Goreng (Indonesian Fried Noodles)

Bami goreng (Indonesische gebakken noedels)Asian dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Bami Goreng (Indonesian Fried Noodles) (250 g) contains 420 kcal, with 59.8 g of net carbohydrates and 15.5 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 55 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 32.9 (High) for its actual effect.

How many calories are in bami goreng?

Bami goreng is the "brother" of nasi goreng: the same idea, but with noodles instead of rice, fried with leek, cabbage and soy sauce. It is one of the best-known dishes of Chinese-Indonesian restaurants in the Netherlands. There are 168 calories per 100 g, and a medium plate (250 g) has about 420 calories, with about 16 g of protein and 11 g of fat. Read also: Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice)

Does bami goreng raise blood sugar?

Noodles made from wheat are gentler on blood sugar than white rice. The closest food that has been measured is udon noodles, with an index of about 55, which means low.

But the amount makes a difference: the glycemic load of a plate is about 33, which means high, because a takeaway plate is usually big.

Bami or nasi?

If you are choosing between the two: bami goreng (55) is gentler on blood sugar than nasi goreng (about 72), and its calories are similar. So if you are watching your blood sugar, bami is the smarter choice.

How to eat it smartly

  • Add more vegetables: ask for more leek, cabbage and green beans.
  • Add more protein: an egg, chicken or prawns keep you fuller.
  • Half a plate with a cucumber salad is enough, and the other half is for tomorrow.
  • Go easy on the sweet ketjap sauce.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of nasi goreng (72): bami goreng (55).
  • Instead of a big plate: half a plate with extra stir-fried vegetables.
