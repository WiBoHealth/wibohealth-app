Puffed Quinoa Cereal

Gepofte quinoa ontbijtgranenGrains & starches

Quick answer

A medium portion of Puffed Quinoa Cereal (30 g) contains 110 kcal, with 17.1 g of net carbohydrates and 4.2 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 65 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 11.1 (Medium) for its actual effect.

Many people look for a quick, crunchy breakfast cereal without additives or hydrogenated oils, and puffed quinoa has become a popular choice in health food shops. Botanically, quinoa is not a true cereal but a pseudo-grain, and one of its main strengths is protein that contains all nine essential amino acids, about 14g per 100g. Puffing with high heat and pressure, however, changes the physical structure of the grain: the starch opens up and is digested as soon as it meets digestive enzymes, so its sugar reaches the blood noticeably faster than that of boiled quinoa.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

A 30g portion looks like a lot in the bowl. It is a good swap for sweetened cornflakes for children, and suits athletes who need easily digested carbohydrate before training.

Best preparation methods

To slow the faster sugar uptake caused by puffing, do not eat it on its own with a thin plant milk; mix in a spoonful of natural peanut butter or sprinkle chia and flaxseed on top to add protein and healthy fats.

Important Nutritional Note

Quinoa is naturally gluten-free, but some factories pack puffed grains on the same lines as wheat. Choose packs with a certified gluten-free label if you have coeliac disease.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Puffed Quinoa Cereal good for weight loss?

    It can be included in moderation: it contains 7 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 30 g portion gives 110 kcal.

    How does Puffed Quinoa Cereal affect blood sugar?

    Its glycemic index is 65 (Medium), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 30 g portion has a glycemic load of 11.1 (Medium), so it is best combined with protein or fibre-rich vegetables.

    What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Puffed Quinoa Cereal?

    A typical 30 g portion of Puffed Quinoa Cereal provides about 110 kcal, 4.2 g protein, 19.2 g carbohydrate, 1.8 g fat and 2.1 g fibre.

    Is puffed quinoa suitable for weight loss and insulin resistance?

    A portion is light (110 kcal in 30g) and gives a great crunch, but puffing gives it a medium glycemic index (65). Eat measured portions and combine them with protein and healthy fats to keep blood sugar steady.
