Goji Berry

Goji bessenDates & dried fruit

Quick answer

A medium portion of Goji Berry (20 g) contains 70 kcal, with 12.8 g of net carbohydrates and 2.9 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 29 (Low) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 3.7 (Low) for its actual effect.

Red berries from traditional Asian medicine, known for their nutrient density and antioxidants such as zeaxanthin. Goji berries have a low glycemic index (≈29) thanks to their complex fibre and protein compared with other dried fruits.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

One tablespoon (about 15 to 20 grams) is plenty to give you a good dose of antioxidants, and eating it with a handful of nuts makes a balanced, energising snack.

Best preparation methods

Soak dried goji berries for a few minutes in warm tea or water to soften them, or sprinkle them over a vegetable salad for a light tang and a cheerful look.

Important Nutritional Note

An excellent choice for supporting eye health and radiant skin thanks to beta-carotene; if you take blood-thinning medication, ask your doctor before eating concentrated amounts every day.

Frequently asked questions

Is Goji Berry good for weight loss?

It can be included in moderation: it contains 13 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 20 g portion gives 70 kcal.

How does Goji Berry affect blood sugar?

Its glycemic index is 29 (Low), a clinically reviewed estimate rather than a laboratory measurement. A typical 20 g portion has a glycemic load of 3.7 (Low), so the effect of this portion on blood sugar is limited.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Goji Berry?

A typical 20 g portion of Goji Berry provides about 70 kcal, 2.9 g protein, 15.4 g carbohydrate, 0.1 g fat and 2.6 g fibre.
