Swedish Cardamom Cinnamon Bun (Kanelbulle)

Sweets & desserts

Quick answer

A medium portion of Swedish Cardamom Cinnamon Bun (Kanelbulle) (90 g) contains 306 kcal, with 46.4 g of net carbohydrates and 6.1 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 65 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 30.1 (High) for its actual effect.

The queen of the Swedish coffee break (fika): rolls of dough scented with ground cardamom, filled with butter and cinnamon and sprinkled with crunchy white pearl sugar; a warm treat to enjoy in moderation because of its high glycemic load.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Cinnamon and cardamom give it a rich flavour, but the amounts in one bun are small and do not offset the sugar and white flour in the dough, so watch the portion.

Best preparation methods

Enjoy it with a black coffee without sugar to balance the sweetness and avoid sugary drinks alongside it.

Important Nutritional Note

For people with diabetes or insulin resistance, it is always recommended to consider the total glycemic load (GL) of the meal rather than relying on the glycemic index alone.
