How many calories are in cinnamon and ginger tea?

The smell of cinnamon with ginger warms the house before it warms your stomach! There are 8 calories per 100 ml, and a cup (240 ml) without sugar has about 19 calories. So it is one of the lightest hot drinks you can have in winter.

Do cinnamon and ginger burn fat?

Since people often search for "cinnamon and ginger for fat burning" and "cinnamon drink for weight loss", let me be honest with you: no drink burns fat on its own.

But the nice thing about it is that it gives you a rich, warm taste without sugar, and that helps you do without sweetened drinks and desserts after meals. That is where the real difference lies on a diet.

Does a cinnamon drink raise blood sugar?

Without sugar, no. It has very little carbohydrate (1.8 g per 100 ml, half a gram of which is fibre), so the glycemic index does not apply to it.

How to make it

  • You can simmer a cinnamon stick with 4 or 5 slices of fresh ginger in two cups of water for 10 minutes over low heat.
  • If you want sweetness, you can add two drops of stevia instead of sugar or honey.
  • A squeeze of lemon at the end gives it a refreshing taste.
  • Ceylon cinnamon tastes milder than the cassia cinnamon that is common in supermarkets.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of a cup of tea with two spoons of sugar (about 32 calories): a cup of cinnamon and ginger without sugar (19 calories).
  • Instead of something sweet after dinner: a hot cup of cinnamon and ginger.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in cinnamon and ginger tea?

8 calories per 100 ml, and about 19 calories per cup, without sugar.

Do cinnamon and ginger burn fat?

No drink burns fat on its own. Its benefit is that it is a warm, light alternative to sweetened drinks.

Does a cinnamon drink raise blood sugar?

Without sugar, no; it has very little carbohydrate.

When is the best time to drink cinnamon and ginger?

There is no magic time, but many people like it after a meal instead of something sweet, or in the evening instead of tea and coffee.
