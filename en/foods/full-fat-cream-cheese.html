How many calories are in Philadelphia cream cheese?

Cream cheese is the secret of cheesecake, bagels and soft sandwiches. There are 342 calories per 100 g, and two tablespoons (30 g) have about 103 calories, most of them from fat (about 10 g).

Does cream cheese raise blood sugar?

No. It has 4.1 g of carbohydrate per 100 g, so the glycemic index does not apply. That is why it is popular on keto.

But is it suitable for weight loss?

Here the story is different: it is true that it does not raise blood sugar, but it is high in calories because it is mostly fat. And spoonfuls of cream cheese slip by easily; you often use more than two.

Also, it has little protein (6 g per 100 g) compared with white cheese or cottage cheese, so it does not fill you up much.

How to eat it smartly

  • Measure the amount with a spoon; don't spread by eye.
  • You can mix it half and half with Greek yoghurt; it cuts the calories and adds protein.
  • With cucumber or celery as a snack, instead of crackers.
  • If you like the taste, there is also a "light" version with less fat.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of full-fat cream cheese: the light version, or labneh.
  • Instead of a bagel with cream cheese: grain bread with a spoon of cream cheese and salmon.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in Philadelphia cream cheese?

342 calories per 100 g, and two tablespoons (30 g) have about 103 calories.

Does cream cheese raise blood sugar?

No, it has little carbohydrate.

Is cream cheese suitable for keto?

Yes, because it is low in carbohydrate. But it is high in calories, so the amount matters.

What is the difference between cream cheese and labneh?

Labneh usually has fewer calories and more protein, while cream cheese is richer and smoother.
