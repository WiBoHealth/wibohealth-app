How many calories are in Kiri cheese?

Little Kiri squares are one of the most loved breakfast cheeses, especially with children. There are 315 calories per 100 g, and one square (about 18 g) has about 57 calories, most of them from fat (about 5 g per square), with little protein (about 1.5 g).

Does Kiri cheese raise blood sugar?

No. It has little carbohydrate (4.5 g per 100 g), so the glycemic index does not apply. The bread you spread it on is what raises blood sugar.

Kiri or cheese triangles?

The question every parent asks 😄 Kiri is richer: per 100 g it has 315 calories and 30 g of fat, while cheese triangles have 230 calories and 18 g of fat. But Kiri feels "creamier" because it contains cream. Read also: Spreadable Cheese Triangles (The Laughing Cow)

Is it suitable for weight loss?

In a small amount, yes. Its advantage is that it comes in squares, so you know what you are eating. But because it is low in protein, it is not very filling, so it is best eaten with something that has protein and vegetables.

How to eat it smartly

  • One square on brown bread with cucumber.
  • With a boiled egg it becomes a balanced breakfast.
  • With apple or cucumber slices as a school snack for children.

Lighter alternatives

  • Instead of two Kiri squares: one square with areesh cheese.
  • If you want a spreadable cheese with fewer calories: labneh, or light cream cheese.
