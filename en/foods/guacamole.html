Guacamole

Verse guacamoleMexican dishes

Quick answer

A medium portion of Guacamole (50 g) contains 75 kcal, with 1 g of net carbohydrates and 1 g of protein.

Traditional Mexican avocado dip is green gold for heart health and metabolism. It is almost free of net carbohydrate and full of healthy monounsaturated fats and fibre, so the glycemic index does not apply, and it gives the body long-lasting fullness and support for steady glucose.

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Use guacamole as a smart, nutritious alternative to mayonnaise and processed sauces in sandwiches and dishes, and enjoy it as a dip with crisp cucumber and carrot slices instead of processed crackers.

Best preparation methods

Mash fresh avocado by hand with a fork with lime juice, chopped tomato, fresh coriander and a little sea salt; the lime helps preserve the antioxidants and stops the avocado from browning.

Important Nutritional Note

Despite its metabolic benefits, its rich healthy fats make it high in calories, so two to three tablespoons are plenty to add fullness and nutrients to a meal.

Frequently asked questions

Is Guacamole good for weight loss?

It can be included in moderation: it contains 7 g of fibre per 100 g, which supports fullness, and a 50 g portion gives 75 kcal.

How does Guacamole affect blood sugar?

Guacamole contains only 2 g of available carbohydrate per 100 g, so the glycemic index does not apply and its direct effect on blood sugar is minimal. The meal’s effect depends on what you eat with it, such as bread, rice or sweetened sauces.

What is the nutritional value of a typical portion of Guacamole?

A typical 50 g portion of Guacamole provides about 75 kcal, 1 g protein, 4.5 g carbohydrate, 7 g fat and 3.5 g fibre.
