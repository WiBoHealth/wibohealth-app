Special Fries (Friet Speciaal)

Fast food

Quick answer

A medium portion of Special Fries (Friet Speciaal) (220 g) contains 715 kcal, with 64.2 g of net carbohydrates and 5.9 g of protein. Its glycemic index is 65 (Medium) for how fast it raises blood sugar, while its glycemic load per portion is 41.8 (High) for its actual effect.

Friet speciaal (or patat speciaal) is one of the most famous dishes of the Dutch snack bar: crisp, salted fries topped with Dutch mayonnaise, sweet spiced curry ketchup and finely chopped raw onion. The combination brings together rich fat, sweet ketchup, warm curry and the bite of onion. In calorie terms, though, it is a very heavy meal: a medium portion (220g) gives about 715 kcal and more than 46g of fat, with a high glycemic load (41.8).

💡 Smart Nutritional Guidance

Consumption advice

Order the small size (kleine friet) or share it with someone, and treat it as a full main meal rather than a light side.

Best preparation methods

Ask for the sauces on the side (apart) where possible, so you dip mindfully instead of drowning the fries in mayonnaise and curry ketchup.

Important Nutritional Note

The glycemic load of a portion is high (41.8): the sugar in curry ketchup and the potato starch, combined with mayonnaise fat, can keep blood sugar and blood fats raised for longer. People with diabetes, insulin resistance or heart disease should be careful and skip sugary drinks with it.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many calories are in a full portion of friet speciaal?

    A medium portion (220g including sauces) contains about 715 kcal: around 470 kcal from the fries and about 240 kcal from the mayonnaise and curry ketchup, which makes it an energy-heavy meal.

    Does the raw onion on friet speciaal add any health benefit?

    Raw onion adds the antioxidant quercetin, natural sulphur compounds and a little fibre, but it does not change the high energy density of the dish.

    What is the difference between friet speciaal and fries with mayo?

    Friet met mayo comes with mayonnaise only, while speciaal adds curry ketchup (which contains sugar and spices) and chopped raw onion, slightly raising the carbohydrate and adding stronger flavour.
