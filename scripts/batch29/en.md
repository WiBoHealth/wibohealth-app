title: Hawawshi Calories: 330 per 100 g – Does Hawawshi Raise Blood Sugar? | WiBo Health
desc: A loaf of hawawshi has about 660 calories! Learn its calories, its effect on blood sugar, and how to make it lighter at home without losing the taste.
refs: The glycemic index of this food is calculated with the FAO/WHO formula for mixed meals: all the available carbohydrate in hawawshi comes from the bread (white Arabic bread 67 ± 5), and the meat contains no carbohydrate. It is not a direct laboratory measurement of hawawshi. Al Dhaheri AS et al. Br J Nutr. 2017;117:1110–7 · FAO/WHO. Carbohydrates in human nutrition. FAO Food and Nutrition Paper 66. Rome; 1998.

### How many calories are in hawawshi?
Hawawshi is the king of Egyptian street food: a baladi loaf stuffed with minced meat, onion, peppers and spices, baked in the oven until it turns crispy and the juices come out 😋 **There are 330 calories per 100 g, and a loaf (about 200 g) has about 660 calories**, with 36 g of protein and 30 g of fat. So one loaf of hawawshi is a full meal and then some.

### Does hawawshi raise blood sugar?
The meat has no carbohydrate, so it all comes down to **the bread**. White Arabic bread has an index of about **67, which means medium**, so we used the same number for hawawshi.
But the amount is the story: **the glycemic load of one loaf is about 42, which means very high.** The fat in the meat slows absorption a little, but it does not cancel the effect.

### Where are the hidden calories?
- **The fat in the minced meat:** the fattier the meat, the more calories.
- **The oil or ghee** brushed on the loaf before baking.
- **The loaf itself**, especially if it is big and well filled.

### How to eat it smartly
- **At home** you can make it with lean minced meat (5–10%) and add more onion, peppers and tomato.
- You can use **brown baladi bread or grain bread** instead of the white loaf.
- **Half a loaf with a big salad** fills you up and cuts the load roughly in half.
- Instead of frying or lots of fat, you can lightly brush the loaf with olive oil.

### Lighter alternatives
- **Instead of a whole loaf of hawawshi:** half a loaf with a green salad and a little tahini.
- **Instead of white bread (67):** grain bread or sourdough bread (47).

### FAQ
**How many calories are in a loaf of hawawshi?**
330 calories per 100 g, and a loaf (about 200 g) has about 660 calories.

**Does hawawshi raise blood sugar?**
Its index is medium (about 67, from the bread), but the load of one loaf is very high (about 42).

**Is hawawshi suitable for weight loss?**
Not as an everyday meal. But you can make it at home with lean meat and brown bread, and eat half a loaf with a salad.

**How do I make a healthier hawawshi at home?**
Lean meat, more vegetables in the filling, brown bread, and a light brush of oil instead of ghee.

### How many calories are in mombar?
Mombar is one of those dishes no Egyptian feast is complete without: casings stuffed with rice, tomato, herbs and spices, boiled and then fried until crispy. **There are 280 calories per 100 g, and a portion (200 g) has about 560 calories**, with 22 g of protein and 24 g of fat.

### Does mombar raise blood sugar?
The casing itself has no carbohydrate, so it is all about the filling. The filling is **white rice**, which has an index of about **73, which means high**, so we used the same number for mombar.
And in terms of amount, **the glycemic load of a portion is about 47, which means very high.** Frying in ghee or oil also adds a clear amount of calories.

### What you should know about mombar
- It is made from **offal**, so it is very important that it is **well cleaned**, from a trusted source, and cooked all the way through.
- Frying after boiling is what gives it its taste, but it is also what adds the fat.

### How to eat it smartly
- **Fry it less:** you can fry it in just one spoon of oil, or in the oven instead of the pan.
- Make it **a side dish** with a salad and soup, not the main dish.
- **3 or 4 pieces are enough**, instead of a full plate.
- You can mix **a little freekeh or bulgur** into the rice filling for more fibre.

### Lighter alternatives
- **Instead of a full plate of mombar:** 3 pieces with a big green salad.
- **Instead of a white rice filling (73):** a filling with bulgur or brown rice (68).

### FAQ
**How many calories are in mombar?**
280 calories per 100 g, and a portion (200 g) has about 560 calories.

**Does mombar raise blood sugar?**
Yes, because it is stuffed with white rice. Its calculated index is about 73 (high), and the load of a portion is high.

**Is mombar suitable for weight loss?**
In a small amount, lightly fried, and with a big salad.

**How do I clean mombar properly?**
With water, salt and vinegar, turning it inside out and rinsing it several times, and it is still best to buy it cleaned from a trusted place.

- You can mix it with low-salt **areesh cheese**, which tones down the saltiness while keeping the taste.
- Drink **enough water** afterwards.

### Lighter alternatives
- **Instead of mish on its own:** half mish and half areesh cheese.
- **Instead of white bread:** brown bread or cucumber slices.

### FAQ

260 calories per 100 g, and a portion (30 g) has about 78 calories.

Its carbohydrate is **low to moderate** (about 11 g per 100 g), coming from **the breadcrumbs**, and the high protein and fat slow absorption. But the fries that usually come with it are what raise the load.

### Where are the hidden calories?
- **Deep-frying:** the breadcrumbs soak up oil.
- **The cheese** melted in the middle.
- **The slice inside:** in the original version it is cured pork (ham), and in other versions turkey or beef. **If the type of meat matters to you, read the package or ask at the restaurant.**

### How to eat it smartly
- **In the oven or an air fryer** instead of deep-frying saves a lot of fat.
- **Low-fat cheese** and a slice of turkey.
- With **a green salad and roasted vegetables** instead of fries.
- **Half a piece** with a big salad is enough.

### Lighter alternatives
- **Instead of fried cordon bleu with fries:** a grilled chicken breast with light cheese on top and a salad.
- **If you want the same idea:** an oven-baked cordon bleu with a slice of turkey.

### FAQ

285 calories per 100 g, and one piece (about 180 g) has about 513 calories.

**Is cordon bleu suitable for weight loss?**
The fried version, not really. But baked in the oven, with light cheese and turkey, and with a salad, it becomes a reasonable choice.
