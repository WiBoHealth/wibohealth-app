The favourite of Belgian and Dutch breakfast tables: it is made by boiling down huge amounts of apples and pears until their natural sugars concentrate into a dark, caramel-like syrup. Even though the jar says "no added sugar", a single spoonful holds concentrated fruit sugar. Its glycemic index is low (estimate) thanks to pectin fibre, but don't pour it on freely.
